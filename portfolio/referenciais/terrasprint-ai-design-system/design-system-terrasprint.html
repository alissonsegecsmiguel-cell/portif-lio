Foundations · 03

Backgrounds

Three grounds: the hero's layered paper with pointer-steered rain (also the live ground of this whole view), the closing section's falling petals, and the page frame that holds everything.

Hero ground · rain

#top · #bloom · canvas#heroParticles (2d)

Layers: shell paper rgb(247,246,239) · charcoal smoke (blur 70px) · green bloom (blur 60px, follows the pointer ±13px / ±10px over 0.7s) · floor haze · rain canvas. Rain: 70 drops (40 under 768px), 12–20px/frame, wind follows the pointer's x, 35% of new drops spawn near the pointer, drops splash on the floor and on the hero's title, tiles, links and risers. Click the ground to open or close the umbrella; it follows the pointer and drops splash on its dome.

Closing ground · petals

footer#contact · canvas#footerParticles (2d)

Petals: 35 quadratic-curve petals in four pinks, gravity 0.8, wind 1.2, sine sway, slow spin; the loop only runs while the section intersects the viewport. Glow: green radial from the bottom edge, blur 60px.

Page frame

body (rgb(234,231,221)) › div.p-2.sm:p-3.lg:p-4 › div.rounded-[28px].sm:rounded-[40px]

The whole page lives inside one rounded paper shell (overflow hidden) inset 8 / 12 / 16px from the ground.
